Delete Your Account

Last updated: May 29, 2026

You can delete your Quippr account and all associated data at any time. Choose one of the options below.

1. Delete in the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the Quippr app:

  1. Open Quippr and sign in to your account
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and associated data will be scheduled for permanent deletion.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

  • Email: hello@quippr.app
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: the email address associated with your Quippr account

We will verify your request and process the deletion for you.

3. What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account and email address
  • All conversation history and messages
  • Statistics, scores, and practice data

All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your deletion request. This action cannot be undone.

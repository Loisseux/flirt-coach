Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2025

Quippr ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Lois Antoine, an individual developer based in France. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the Quippr mobile application.

1. Data We Collect

  • Email address — collected at registration for authentication purposes
  • Conversation content — messages you send within the app are stored to provide conversation history and statistics
  • Usage data — scores, scenarios used, and practice streaks to provide statistics

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Quippr service
  • To save your conversation history and statistics
  • To authenticate your account
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising purposes

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — used for authentication and data storage. Data is stored on secure servers in the European Union.
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — AI provider that powers the chat feature. Messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by contacting us at the email below.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — you can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can request deletion of your data at any time
  • Right to portability — you can request your data in a portable format
  • Right to object — you can object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: hello@quippr.app

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with encryption at rest.

7. Children's Privacy

Quippr is intended for users aged 17 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 17. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or by email.
